# Changelog

## 0.9.0 (2026-10-24)

First public release.

- 15 remix styles: slowed + reverb, sped up, nightcore, daycore, vaporwave, 8D, bass boosted, lo-fi, bathroom at a party, sunday night, underwater, empty arena, in the rain, ethereal and phonk, each with soft, standard and intense versions (8D picks its path instead).
- Custom panel with speed, bass, pitch, saturation, tape, chorus, vinyl, reverb, room, 8D, rain and water controls, and saved styles.
- Effects on protected services, such as Spotify and Amazon Music, through Chrome tab capture.
- One tab at a time: other tabs keep their original sound.
- Stage with the arabot character, an animated scene per style and a visualizer that follows the music.
- Track and artist from the site's media information, scrolling behind the character when they do not fit.
- Interface in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, following the browser language.
- Firefox 128 or newer, built by `npm run package` as `dist/auracore-firefox.zip`; protected services get speed and pitch only there.
- Support panel with Ko-fi, and Pix in Brazilian Portuguese.
